## Proposed first paid stage

Request **one cold gate invocation**, then stop for evidence review. Retain the
ordinary 75-minute admission-through-settlement ceiling, at most 40 minutes of
preparation, and the existing cleanup allowance inside a fixed two-hour outer
reservation. This is a cost ceiling, not a runtime estimate or a higher timeout.
A failed preparation ends the reservation and preserves its records; a launched
gate consumes attempt 10 even if incomplete. No automatic retry, warm run,
optimization, replacement, or extension follows.

The current ordinary collector schedules nine slots per revision. It must not be
started under this one-invocation proposal without a reviewed enforcing one-slot
controller/entry. This plan deliberately does not smuggle the earlier special
initial-three controller or its authority into the new policy. Selecting and
implementing the approved staged protocol is a remaining launch prerequisite.

Expected outcomes are bounded and useful:

- Complete gate and complete telemetry: inspect cost boundaries and representative
  exposure before deciding whether a matched campaign is affordable. One sample
  cannot establish a performance improvement.
- Complete gate with a proved optional timeout: retain root success, the failed
  helper custody, stage boundaries and incomplete telemetry. The measurement is
  unaccepted; no reuse or next slot follows. Investigate the identified interval.
- Test failure, mandatory sensor/identity/storage failure, cancellation, unknown
  custody, or overall deadline: cancel and retain evidence. Fix the concrete issue
  before proposing a newly charged attempt.
- Cleanup cannot be proved: preserve owner/guards/products and stop. No retry or
  reclamation is permitted by process absence or a quiet log alone.

## Cost of a later comparison

The unchanged complete protocol requires nine baseline and nine optimization
slots: twelve cold/warm gates capped at 75 minutes, six reuse observations capped
at 10 minutes, ten hours per revision and twenty hours overall including reserved
preparation and overhead. The gate allowances alone total sixteen hours. These
are maxima, not predictions; prior failures do not support a credible runtime
estimate. Only one optimization is in scope, with matched controls and complete
exposure review.

Eighteen additional invocations plus the nine already charged would total 27,
which exceeds the earlier cumulative cap of 20. A full new comparison therefore
requires an explicit revised attempt budget or a separately reviewed smaller
experimental design. Do not reset the count by choosing a new root. Any smaller
design must state its weaker inference and may require different controller and
analysis tests. No attempt budget is increased by this document.

At each stop, retain the exact input identities, started/finished slot records,
root exit and settlement, helper custody and boundary traces, completeness,
observed exposure, test/leg coverage, storage events and remaining budget. Report
partial results plainly. Only a separately authorized release workflow can
produce deployment or production acceptance evidence.
