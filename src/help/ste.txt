STE writing guidance for story text

Write titles, descriptions, plans, and comments in ASD-STE100 Simplified
Technical English. Keep sentences short and clear. Use direct verbs and
consistent technical terms. Preserve meaning and literal evidence.

StoryHook does not run STE lint validation or add grammar advice. This applies
to all callers: operators, agents, automation, and callers without actor labels.
Sentence length, contractions, vocabulary, and passive voice do not reject a write.
Other command validation and transaction safeguards still apply.

Record approved plans verbatim. No special STE wrapper or rewrite is required.
Keep code, command output, paths, identifiers, and quoted evidence accurate.
Existing history, restoration, and replay preserve the original text.

Agents must continue to follow this writing guidance. Authors remain responsible
for meaning, clarity, grammar, and technical terminology.

Standard: https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf
